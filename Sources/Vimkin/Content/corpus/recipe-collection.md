# Family Recipe Collection — The Keepers

Recipes that survived at least three attempts and one skeptical
dinner guest. Measurements are honest, timings are real.

## Tuesday Lentil Soup

Serves 4, or 2 with excellent leftovers.

**You need:**

- 1 cup brown lentils, rinsed
- 1 onion, chopped small
- 2 carrots, chopped however
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 can (400g) crushed tomatoes
- 5 cups vegetable broth
- 1 teaspoon cumin (we have plenty)
- Salt, pepper, a squeeze of lemon at the end

**Steps:**

1. Soften the onion and carrots in olive oil, about 8 minutes.
2. Add garlic and cumin, stir for 1 minute until it smells right.
3. Add lentils, tomatoes, and broth. Simmer 30 minutes.
4. Salt at the end, not the start. Lemon just before serving.

The lemon is not optional. We have tested this.

## Renata's Weekend Bread

One loaf. Start the night before — the dough does the work.

**You need:**

- 3 cups bread flour
- 1.5 teaspoons salt
- Half a teaspoon of dry yeast
- 1.5 cups cool water

**Steps:**

1. Stir everything into a shaggy mess. Cover the bowl.
2. Leave it alone on the counter overnight, 12 to 16 hours.
3. Next day: fold it over itself four times, rest 30 minutes.
4. Bake in a covered pot at 230°C: 22 minutes lid on,
   18 minutes lid off. Trust the sticky note.
5. Wait one full hour before slicing. Yes, a whole hour.

## Emergency Chocolate Mug Cake

For emergencies. Two minutes, one mug, zero judgment.

1. In a big mug: 4 tablespoons flour, 2 of cocoa, 2 of sugar,
   a pinch of salt, a quarter teaspoon of baking powder.
2. Stir in 3 tablespoons milk and 1 of neutral oil.
3. Microwave 70 to 90 seconds. It should look barely set on top.
4. Eat directly from the mug. That is the entire point.
