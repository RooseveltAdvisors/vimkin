# Week of March 9 — Journal

## Monday

Slow start. The coffee maker made a noise like a small motorcycle
and then gave up entirely. Drank tea and pretended to like it.

Finished the library book about lighthouses. The keeper in chapter
nine kept a journal for forty years and never missed a day. I have
missed four days this month. He wins.

## Tuesday

Walked the long way to the market and saw the heron again, standing
in the drainage pond like it owns the place. It probably does.

Bought too many oranges because they were on sale. This is a
recurring theme in my life and I have decided to accept it.

## Wednesday

Called Mom. She has opinions about the neighbor's new fence, and
now, somehow, so do I. Forty-five minutes about a fence.

Evening: tried the bread recipe again. Third attempt. The crust
finally cracked the way it does in the pictures. Wrote the exact
oven timing on a sticky note before I forget it: 22 minutes lid
on, 18 minutes lid off.

## Thursday

Rain all day. Good rain, the steady kind. Cleaned out the desk
drawer and found three dead pens, a movie ticket from 2019, and
the spare key I bought a replacement for in January.

## Friday

Lunch with Renata downtown. She's leaving the bakery to start her
own thing — a little stall at the weekend market first, then who
knows. She looked terrified and happy at the same time. I told her
that's probably the correct combination.

## Weekend notes

- Fix or replace the coffee maker. This is not optional.
- Return the lighthouse book, pick up the sequel if there is one.
- Oranges: eat them. All of them. No new oranges.
- Write down the fence opinion so I can let go of it.

Small week. Good week.
