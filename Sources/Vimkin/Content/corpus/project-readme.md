# The Pantry Ledger

A little system for keeping track of what's in the pantry, so we
stop buying a fourth jar of cumin while owning zero rice.

## What this is

A shared notebook (this folder) where every shelf gets a page and
every page gets updated on grocery day. That's it. No app, no
subscription, no scanning barcodes at 10 pm.

## How it works

1. Before shopping, open the page for each shelf.
2. Cross off anything we ran out of during the week.
3. The shopping list is everything crossed off, plus the meal plan.
4. After shopping, write the new items in. Date the big stuff.

## The pages

- `shelf-top.md` — baking things, the mixer, the good chocolate
- `shelf-middle.md` — cans, jars, pasta, the cumin collection
- `shelf-bottom.md` — heavy stuff: oil, vinegar, the rice we now own
- `freezer.md` — labeled bags only. Mystery containers get one month.
- `spices.md` — alphabetical, or at least alphabetical in spirit

## Rules we actually follow

- If you open the last one, you write it down. No exceptions.
- Leftovers get a name and a date. "Soup?" is not a name.
- The good chocolate is for baking. (This rule is aspirational.)

## Rules we have quietly abandoned

- Weighing the flour jar to estimate remaining flour
- The color-coded sticker system
- Meal-planning more than four days ahead

## Frequently asked questions

**Why not use an app?**
We tried two. Both wanted an account, one wanted a subscription,
and neither knew what to do about the cumin.

**Who is in charge of the ledger?**
Whoever shopped last. Power transfers with the receipt.

**What happens if the ledger is wrong?**
We eat something creative on Thursday. The system is self-correcting.

## Status

Running since February. Cumin jars acquired since then: zero.
Rice supply: stable. The system works.
