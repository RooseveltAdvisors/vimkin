# moving house - the everything list

boxes needed: eleven, probably fifteen
tape: more than you think
then more
label the box with the kettle in it

# the rooms
  the small room is easy
  and will lie to you about the rest
  the kitchen is nine boxes in a coat
the bookshelf is not furniture
it is a decision.

# the day itself
feed whoever helps you, first not last
keep one bag: a towel, a charger
and something sweet
do not open a box on the first night

# after
the house feels wrong for eleven days
then it does not
hang one picture early. any picture.
