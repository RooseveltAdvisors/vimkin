# Moving house - the everything list

Boxes needed: eleven, probably fifteen.
Tape: more than you think. Always more than you think.
Label the box with the kettle in it. Label it twice.

# The rooms

The small room is easy and will lie to you about the rest.
The kitchen is nine boxes wearing a trench coat.
The bookshelf is not a piece of furniture, it is a decision.

# The day itself

Feed whoever helps you. Feed them first, not last.
Keep one bag with a towel, a charger and something sweet.
Do not open a single box on the first night.

# After

The house will feel wrong for eleven days and then it will not.
Hang one picture early. Any picture. It tells the walls who lives here.
