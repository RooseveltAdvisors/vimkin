# the desk at midnight

the lamp buzzed twice
and the dark leaned in.
listen.
I found a candle stub in the drawer
wax, three matches, one worm-track

# the wide margin
  nothing grows out here
  only ruled lines and quiet

a small thing is breathing
under this page all night
hush.
the Worm does not breathe
