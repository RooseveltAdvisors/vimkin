# Packing list - three days on the coast

socks wool thick and grey
lamp small brass hooded
notebook the green one please
pen black ink not blue
map folded twice and softened

The Worm ate every comma in this list.
Now each word stands alone on the shore.
Step stone to stone and nobody gets wet.
